## ABC Analysis  
ABC analysis is based on the idea that a relatively small number of products can account for a large proportion of sales value.

A common approach is:  
### A products

Approximately the first 80% of cumulative revenue

These are the products that deserve close monitoring.

### B products

Approximately 80–95%

Moderate importance.

### C products

Approximately 95–100%

Lower contribution to total revenue.

These percentages are a practical convention, not a universal rule. A business can choose different thresholds.  


#### The sheet answers one simple management question:

“Which products contribute most to total retail revenue?”  

Then your ABC table answers:  

Class --->	Meaning  
A	---> Highest cumulative revenue contribution  
B	---> Moderate contribution  
C	---> Lower contribution
